Calibration and channel quality

Calibrate to select the RTF channel pair.

Pulse fit measures each channel against a repeating pulse model. Pair agreement measures similarity between channels. Neither percentage is measurement accuracy. Slow (<0.5 Hz) and high-frequency (>8 Hz) content describe detrended signal energy; they are not a validated noise or accuracy score.
